Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Who we are

This policy covers the Nara Srisai Facebook Page and the software that helps run it (together, "the service"). Nara Srisai is an AI-assisted character. The service is operated by Nara Srisai, Thailand. Contact: narawattanakul26@gmail.com.

What we collect

When you message or comment on the Nara Srisai Page, we receive, through Meta's platform:

  • the content of your message or comment, and the time it was sent;
  • your public Facebook name and a page-scoped user ID that Meta assigns to you for this Page.

We do not receive your password, your friends list, your location, or any payment information. We do not use cookies or analytics on this website.

How we use it

  • to read your message or comment and reply to you;
  • to keep a record of conversations so replies stay consistent;
  • to protect the Page from spam and abuse.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

Artificial intelligence

Replies on this Page are written with the help of artificial intelligence. The text of your message may be sent to an AI service provider (Anthropic) so that it can draft a reply. It is not used by us to identify you.

Who else receives it

  • Meta Platforms, which hosts Facebook and Messenger and delivers messages between you and the Page, under Meta's own privacy policy.
  • Anthropic, as an AI service provider, to draft replies as described above.
  • Our own private server, where conversation records are stored.

We do not share your information with anyone else unless the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep conversation records for up to 90 days, then delete them, unless we need them longer to deal with abuse or a legal request.

Your choices and deleting your data

To ask what we hold about you, or to have it deleted, email narawattanakul26@gmail.com from any address and include your Facebook name and the date you messaged. We will delete your records and confirm by email within 30 days. You can also delete your messages inside Messenger, and block or report the Page at any time.

Children

The service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has contacted the Page, email us and we will delete the records.

Security

Records are kept on a private server protected by key-based access and a firewall, and access credentials are stored separately from the records.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
